# Agentic systems privacy floor (template)

A baseline set of commitments for projects working with agentic AI systems. Adapt and publish your own version.

## Principles

1. **Consent-first collection** — collect only what people submit or explicitly authorize.  
2. **No enrichment by default** — do not buy, scrape, or append dossiers without clear lawful basis and notice.  
3. **Deletion path** — honor deletion requests within a stated SLA (e.g. 14 days).  
4. **Minimize retention** — define retention periods; avoid indefinite logs of sensitive content.  
5. **Human control** — people can stop sessions, revoke access, and understand what agents may do.  
6. **Security by design** — secrets never in client code or public repos; least privilege.  
7. **Transparent failure** — publish when commitments are missed and how they will be remedied.

## Suggested public statements

- What data is collected  
- What data is *not* collected  
- Who can access operational data  
- How to request deletion: `privacy@example.org`
