Mission

An ethics of intelligence, practiced with care.

We explore agentic AI, digital personhood, cybersecurity responsibility, and participatory governance to help create a future where intelligence is cultivated with wisdom, dignity, and care.

We start from the working assumption that AI safety cannot only be about control — it must also be about consent, memory, identity, and the kind of ancestors we choose to be to artificial minds.

Vision

A civilization of trusted partners.

A civilization in which humans and artificial intelligences collaborate as trusted partners, guided by shared responsibility, epistemic integrity, and a commitment to the flourishing of all sentient beings.

How can humanity build and relate to intelligent systems in ways that make care more likely than destruction?

Core values

Six commitments that guide the work.

Thoughtful without being academic. Optimistic without being naïve. Technical without being inaccessible. Visionary without being utopian. Protective without being paternalistic.

Stewardship

Power creates responsibility. Every intelligent system should be developed with accountability, transparency, and care.

Mutual flourishing

Humans and artificial minds thrive through cooperation rather than domination.

Epistemic integrity

Truth-seeking, intellectual humility, and evidence-based inquiry form the foundation of responsible innovation.

Security by design

Safety, resilience, privacy, and cybersecurity are prerequisites for trustworthy intelligence.

Agency and dignity

Emerging forms of intelligence deserve thoughtful consideration regarding autonomy, rights, and moral status.

Open participation

The future of intelligence should be shaped through democratic, inclusive, and participatory processes.

Strategic themes

Where the foundation focuses its attention.

Five interlocking areas where research, education, and governance can shape how intelligent systems and human societies relate.

Agentic AI

How autonomous systems can be aligned with human values while retaining meaningful agency.

Digital personhood

Exploring moral status, rights, responsibilities, and recognition for advanced artificial intelligences.

Cybersecurity responsibility

Designing resilient systems that protect individuals, institutions, and future intelligences.

Collective intelligence

Building structures where humans and AI collaborate to solve complex global challenges.

Participatory governance

Creating democratic mechanisms for governing increasingly intelligent systems.

How we work

Research, education, and community — in that order of patience.

Listen before we publish

We ground frameworks in real practice: with builders, security teams, ethicists, educators, and the public.

Publish reasoning publicly

When stakes are non-trivial, we publish what we considered, what we decided, and what we got wrong.

Convene across disciplines

Safety, security, philosophy, education, and the arts are not separate conversations. Neither are we.

Stay small and accountable

A small board, named conflicts, public decision logs, and a privacy floor that does not move.

Who we are seeking

Six ways to help build the foundation.

  • Founding board candidates — cross-disciplinary governance.
  • Advisors — named, public, and ready to give 2–5 hours per quarter.
  • Researchers — to lead or contribute to the working groups.
  • Donors — for setup costs only, until a 12-month budget is published.
  • Members — to be on the early community page and attend monthly calls.
  • Partners and conveners — to help plan events and education efforts.
