What we ask of everyone

A short, mutual agreement.

Bring a question, not just a résumé

The strongest signal of fit is a question you cannot put down. Tell us what you think Guardian Builder must ask well.

Consent before contact

We never add you to a list you did not opt into. The form requires an explicit consent-to-contact checkbox.

Privacy, default

We do not sell, rent, or enrich your contact data. You can ask us to delete your record at any time.
